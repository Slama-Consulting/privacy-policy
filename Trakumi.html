Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 19, 2026

SLAMA Consulting ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Trakumi mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our app. By using Trakumi, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • First name and last name
  • Email address
  • Date of birth
  • Gender
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Profile photo (optional)

1.2 Physical and Health Data

To provide fitness tracking features, we collect:

  • Weight and height
  • Fitness level
  • Calories burned (calculated from your activity and profile data)
  • Heart rate data (when provided by a connected device)
  • VO2max estimates (calculated from your activity data)

1.3 Location Data

During active sport tracking sessions, we collect precise GPS data including:

  • Latitude and longitude coordinates
  • Altitude
  • Speed
  • GPS accuracy
  • Timestamps for each recorded point

Location data is only collected when you actively start a tracking session. We do not track your location in the background.

1.4 Activity Data

  • Sport type selected
  • Distance traveled
  • Duration of sessions
  • Average and maximum speed
  • Pace and elevation changes
  • Weather conditions at the time of your activity (temperature, weather code)

1.5 User-Generated Content

  • Chat messages within event groups and club groups
  • Event and club descriptions, titles, photos
  • Photos uploaded for profile, events, clubs, chats, or social posts
  • AI Coach conversations (stored locally on your device only)

1.6 Device and Technical Data

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token for push notifications
  • Device model and operating system version (via crash reporting)
  • App performance metrics (startup time, network latency)
  • Advertising ID (Android) for ad personalization, subject to your consent

1.7 Usage Data

  • Screen views and navigation patterns
  • Feature usage and interactions (e.g., button taps, sport selections)
  • Crash logs and error reports

1.8 Safety Contacts

You may optionally add emergency contacts in the app. Safety contacts are stored locally in the encrypted on-device database and synchronized to your private Firestore account so they remain available across your devices. Safety contacts are never shared with other users.

1.9 Social Feed

If you choose to publish posts to the Social Feed, the following information is stored in Firestore and visible to other Trakumi users: your display name and profile photo, post text, attached photos or trip snapshots, likes, and comments. You can delete your own posts at any time.

1.10 Referral Program Data

When you generate or redeem a referral code, we store: the referral code, the referrer user ID, the referred user ID, and the redemption timestamp. This data is used solely to grant Premium rewards and to detect abuse of the referral program.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide core features: Track your sport activities, calculate performance metrics, and display your history
  • Enable social features: Allow you to participate in community events, join clubs, post to the Social Feed, chat with other users, and appear on leaderboards
  • Personalize your experience: Calculate calories based on your physical profile, suggest relevant sports, and customize your dashboard
  • Power the AI Coach: Send your activity data and messages to Google Gemini to provide personalized coaching, training plans, and post-trip analysis
  • Send notifications: Inform you about event invitations, chat messages, club updates, challenge reminders, and training session reminders
  • Improve the app: Analyze usage patterns, identify bugs, and optimize performance
  • Display advertisements: Show personalized ads through Google AdMob (with your consent where required by law)
  • Ensure safety: Detect cheating in sport tracking, moderate chat content, and enforce community guidelines

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect and process your data:

3.1 Firebase (Google)

  • Firebase Authentication: Manages user accounts and authentication, including Google Sign-In
  • Cloud Firestore: Stores user data, trips, events, clubs, messages, and social posts
  • Firebase Storage: Stores uploaded photos (profile, events, clubs, social feed)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: Delivers push notifications
  • Firebase Analytics: Collects usage statistics
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Collects crash reports for debugging
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring: Monitors app performance
  • Firebase Cloud Functions: Validates in-app purchase receipts and handles server-side logic

For more information, see Firebase Privacy Policy.

3.2 Google AdMob

We display advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (Advertising ID), interaction data, and IP addresses. Ad personalization is subject to your GDPR consent choices, which we collect using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can change your consent at any time via the "Privacy Settings" option in the app.

For more information, see Google Privacy Policy.

3.3 Google Maps

We use Google Maps SDK to display your GPS tracks and event locations on maps. Google Maps may collect usage data according to its own privacy policy.

3.4 Open-Meteo

We use the Open-Meteo API to fetch weather information during your sport sessions. Your GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude) are sent to Open-Meteo to retrieve local weather data. Open-Meteo is a free, open-source weather API.

3.5 Google Play Billing

In-app purchases (Premium subscription) are processed through Google Play Billing. Payment information is handled entirely by Google Play.

3.6 Google Gemini AI (AI Coach & Sport Story)

The AI Coach, the post-trip analysis, and the Sport Story features rely on Google's Gemini API. When you interact with these features, the following data may be sent to Google's Gemini service:

  • Your messages and questions to the AI Coach
  • Your activity summary (distance, duration, average pace, sport type, heart rate summary) for post-trip analysis and Sport Story generation
  • Your profile context (age range, fitness level, training goals) to personalize responses

AI Coach conversations are stored locally on your device only and are not synchronized to Firestore. Generated Sport Story narratives are cached both locally and in Firestore (linked to your account). Daily generation limits apply. For more information, see Google Gemini API Terms.

3.7 Wear OS Data Layer

If you use the Trakumi Wear OS companion app on a paired Wear OS smartwatch, GPS coordinates and trip data are exchanged between your phone and your watch through Google's Wearable Data Layer API. Data remains on your personal devices and is not sent to third parties by this channel. When a trip is completed on the watch, it is relayed to the phone and then stored and synchronized like any other trip.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Firebase cloud services (hosted by Google) and locally on your device using encrypted storage. We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • All network communications use HTTPS encryption
  • Firebase Authentication secures user accounts
  • Firestore Security Rules restrict data access
  • Local Room database is excluded from Android backup and device transfer
  • Screen capture is blocked in release builds
  • API keys (Google Maps, Gemini, AdMob) are injected at build time and not bundled in source

5. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Your data is retained as long as your account is active
  • Deleted accounts: When you delete your account, your data is soft-deleted and may be permanently removed within 30 days
  • Trip data: GPS points and trip records are retained until you manually delete them or delete your account
  • Chat messages (events, clubs): Messages are retained within their associated events or clubs until the event / club / message is deleted
  • AI Coach conversations: Stored locally on your device only; deleted when you clear the AI Coach history or uninstall the app
  • Crash and analytics data: Retained according to Firebase default retention policies (typically 90 days for crash data)

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update your personal information through the app's profile editor
  • Deletion: Delete your account and associated data from within the app
  • Consent withdrawal: Modify your advertising consent at any time through the app's Privacy Settings
  • Data portability: Export your trip data as PDF or GPX/KML from the app

To exercise these rights, use the in-app features or contact us at the address below.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain consent for personalized advertising. You can manage your consent preferences at any time through the "Privacy Settings" option in your profile.

  • Right to Know: You have the right to request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the sources of collection, the business purposes, and the categories of third parties with whom we share it.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request deletion of your personal information. You can delete your account directly within the app through the Profile settings.
  • Right to Correct: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate personal information. You can update your profile information directly in the app.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing: We do not sell your personal information in the traditional sense. However, our use of Google AdMob for personalized advertising may constitute "sharing" under the CCPA/CPRA. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through the "Privacy Settings" option in your profile.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA/CPRA rights.

To exercise these rights, use the in-app features or contact us at slama.walid1985@gmail.com. We will respond to verifiable requests within 45 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Trakumi is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected data from a child under 13, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

10. Clubs

Clubs are user-created groups centered around a shared sport interest. A club profile contains a name, description, optional cover photo, and member list. Clubs may host their own events and challenges. Photos uploaded to a club are stored in Firebase Storage and visible to club members. Joining or leaving a club, as well as deleting a club you own, can be done from the app at any time.

11. Chat & Community Moderation

To ensure a safe community experience, we implement the following moderation features across events, clubs, and the Social Feed:

  • Users can report inappropriate messages, posts, or profiles with a reason
  • Users can block other users to hide their messages, posts, and comments
  • Automated moderation: users who receive multiple reports may have their messages removed and be muted in events or clubs
  • Severely reported accounts may be suspended

Report data (including the reported content and reason) is stored to enable moderation. Users can delete their own messages and posts at any time.

12. Referral Program

Trakumi provides an optional referral program: each user has a unique referral code that can be shared with friends. When a new user registers using a valid referral code, both the referrer and the referred user receive a 7-day Premium trial. We store the minimum necessary data to grant rewards and prevent abuse (such as creating multiple accounts with the same device). Referral rewards are not transferable and may be withheld if abuse is detected.

13. Sport Story

The Sport Story feature uses Google Gemini AI to generate a narrative of your athletic journey based on your trip history, performance data, and fitness level. Generated stories are cached locally and in Firestore. Shared story images contain only the performance statistics that are visible on the image you share. Daily generation limits apply. This feature is available to Premium subscribers or by watching a rewarded advertisement.

They do not constitute professional medical or fitness advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting or modifying any exercise program. SLAMA Consulting is not liable for any injury, health issue, or adverse outcome resulting from the use of these estimates or recommendations.

15. Event and Training Reminders

Trakumi sends push notifications for event invitations, event reminders (typically 3 hours before a scheduled event), club updates, challenge reminders, and training session reminders from your personalized training plan. Delivery of these notifications relies on the POST_NOTIFICATIONS Android permission and may be affected by device-specific battery optimization settings. You can disable any category of reminder at any time in the app's Settings.

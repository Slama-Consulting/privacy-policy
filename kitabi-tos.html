2 Description of Service

Kitabi is a Quran companion mobile application that provides the following features:

  • Quran Reading: Read the Quran with multiple display modes (Mushaf page, Surah reader), bookmarks, and reading progress tracking
  • Hifz (Memorization) Tracking: Track your Quran memorization progress with spaced repetition review scheduling, session management, and mastery levels
  • AI Search: Search the Quran and Hadith using AI-powered natural language queries (powered by Google Gemini)
  • Tajweed Analysis: Record your Quran recitation and receive AI-powered Tajweed feedback and analysis
  • Quranic Healing (Shifa): Track your emotional well-being through mood entries and journal notes, and receive personalized Quranic verse prescriptions generated by AI
  • Tafsir: Access Quran commentary and interpretation
  • Gamification: Earn XP, badges, maintain activity streaks, compete on leaderboards, and earn Hasanat (in-app currency) through interactive Quran games
  • Cloud Sync: Synchronize your progress across multiple devices (subscription required)

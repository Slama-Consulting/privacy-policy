2 Description of Service

Kitabi is a Quran companion mobile application that provides the following features:

  • Quran Reading: Read the Quran (surah, juz and page views) with translations, transliteration, recitation audio, bookmarks and reading progress
  • Hifz (Memorization): Track your memorization with guided sessions, spaced-repetition reviews and progress tracking
  • Recitation practice (Test): Recite an ayah and get a score. Your voice is transcribed by your device's speech recognition service; it is not analysed by AI and not stored by us
  • AI Search: Ask questions about the Quran and Hadith in natural language (powered by Google Gemini)
  • Shifa: Choose how you feel and receive Quranic verses, hadiths and duas selected inside the app for that emotion; keep a personal reflection journal
  • Tafsir: Quran commentary (subscription required)
  • Games and gamification: Quran games, XP, levels, badges, streaks, leaderboard, and Hasanat (in-app virtual currency)
  • Cloud backup: Synchronize your progress across devices (subscription required)

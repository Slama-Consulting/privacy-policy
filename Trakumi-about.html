About Trakumi

Trakumi is a modern multi-sport tracking companion built for athletes who want more than just a GPS recorder. It combines accurate tracking with AI coaching, training plans, and a lightweight social layer.

Key Features

Accurate GPS Tracking Record distance, pace, elevation, heart rate, and weather for every session. Works offline.
AI Coach Chat with an AI coach, get personalized training plans, and receive a post-trip analysis after each session.
Sport Story Turn your activity history into a shareable AI-generated narrative.
Events & Clubs Join or create local events and clubs, chat with other athletes, and take on challenges.
Social Feed Share your best trips, photos, and milestones with the community.
Wear OS Companion Pair Trakumi with a Wear OS watch to track sessions directly from your wrist.
Safety Contacts Configure emergency contacts that stay securely on your account.
Export & Share Export your trips as PDF, GPX, or KML and share summaries with friends.

Support

Need help? Here is how to get it:

  • In-app settings: Most common issues (notifications not arriving, tracking not starting) can be solved by checking permissions and battery optimization in the Settings screen of the app.
  • Email support: Reach us directly at slama.walid1985@gmail.com. Please include your device model, Android version, and the Trakumi version (visible in Profile → About).
  • Response time: We aim to respond within a few business days. Complex technical issues may take longer.
